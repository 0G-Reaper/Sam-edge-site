# Discord rollout messages

Prepared drafts. Not posted. Publish only through the authorized official bot after connectivity and member channel permissions are verified. Space updates naturally; publish a milestone once, with its actual evidence. Do not convert these into announcements that a pending feature is live.

## Development teaser — usable before activation

We’re building the next part of SAM around a simple idea: an invitation gets you in. Useful research builds your reputation.

Private member profiles, ten invitations per member, research quests and a limited founder collection are in development. The goal is a community where a strong question—and the evidence behind it—matters more than the loudest prediction.

We’ll show what works as it passes testing.

## Evidence-review preview — only after the worker integration is tested

A confident paragraph isn’t enough.

SAM’s research inbox is being designed to separate what a source supports, what remains uncertain and what contradicts the claim. Contributions that pass the first checks can move to a deeper review. Points follow that review; posting more words does not earn them.

The useful part is the record: what was submitted, what was checked and why the conclusion changed.

## Collection preview — artwork finished, issuance not yet launched

First Man on the Moon. Stargazer. Techhead. Wolf of Wall Street.

The first collection artwork is ready. The planned limits are 200 founder badges and 100 Discord pioneer choices across the three character designs. Your profile will show serial numbers and the remaining allocation when membership launches.

These are community collectibles, not investments or a promise of financial value.

## Activation announcement — only after all named features work live

SAM’s member community is open through personal invitations.

Existing early-access members can verify their registered email. Each verified member receives ten invitations, and the first quest is to welcome someone they trust. Your profile tracks progress, community standing and research rank separately.

Discord verification comes next. Bring curiosity. Bring sources. Be willing to change your mind.
